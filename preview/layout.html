The spacing scale stops at 24. Anything larger in the app is a one-off literal at the call site, not a token. If a sixth step is genuinely needed, add it to Spacing.swift first.
Cards are 16, buttons are 12. A prominent primary CTA uses Radius.button; a non-prominent one switches to Radius.pill. That swap is the only place radius carries meaning. Tag chips are 12 — not pills.
