Those canonical flags are recent; a stale simulator build will silently ignore them and hand you identical screenshots, which is worth checking before trusting a capture.
Where source and screen disagreed, the screen won and is noted on the card. Two cases so far: the page ground is muted rather than background, and the CTA gradient does not change between themes.
