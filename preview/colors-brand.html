Never use primary for text. At 2.6:1 on white it fails AA outright. Small orange text uses primaryText; a filled button with a white label uses primaryButton; primary is for fills, icons and accents.
The CTA gradient matches none of them. #F56B0F → #F16000 lives in PrimaryCTAFill.swift and was verified on screen at #F46B10 → #F26203. Rebuilding a CTA from a flat primary fill loses both contrast and the inner highlight.
