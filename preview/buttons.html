The CTA gradient is theme-invariant. Sampled at #F46B10 → #F26203 in light and #F56B10 → #F26102 in dark — the same. It never uses primaryButton's dark value. A dark mockup that darkens the CTA is wrong.
Prominence changes the radius, not the colour. Prominent uses Radius.button (12); non-prominent switches to Radius.pill. Same fill either way.
Press is 0.98 over 120ms with a .selection haptic. Gentle on purpose — .impact was judged too loud for buttons that already animate.
