primaryText inverts rather than darkens. Light uses the deep #C2410C because the true brand orange only reaches 2.6:1 on white. Dark uses the true brand orange #FF7B23, which clears AA there at ~6.3:1. Dark is the only place the brand orange is legal as text.
The CTA gradient does not change. Verified on screen at #F46B10 → #F26203 in light and #F56B10 → #F26102 in dark — the same. PrimaryCTAFill never uses primaryButton's dark value.
In dark the border carries the edges, not the shadow. The white glow was dimmed 8% → 5% because it read as a halo, and border was brightened to #475569 to compensate. Dropping the border in dark leaves cards undefined.
